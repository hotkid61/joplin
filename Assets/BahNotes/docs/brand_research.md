# Brand research notes (public sources)

Collected for the Booz Allen Notes rebrand. **Not a substitute for the internal Brand Guide.**

## Findings

1. **No public self-serve brand PDF** with downloadable logo packs was found on boozallen.com. Graphic/design roles reference internal “brand management guidelines.”
2. **Modern public identity** (logo history write-ups): laconic **black/white** wordmark; name set in title case geometric sans; three parts often separated by vertical bars (`Booz | Allen | Hamilton`).
3. **Cited digital palette** (third-party, marked unverified by ColorCodeGuide):
   - BAH Blue `#004C97`
   - BAH Dark Gray `#333333` (PMS 447 C cited)
4. **Typography**: compared by third parties to Franklin Gothic–like faces; exact corporate font is not published for free use.
5. **Press / media**: Media Center and Media Relations are the public routes for press assets — not a free logo CDN for product forks.
